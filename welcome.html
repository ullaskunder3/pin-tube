Pin-Tube is installed!

Pin videos while watching, find them on your homepage later.

1

Pin the extension for easy access

Click the puzzle piece icon in the Chrome toolbar, then click the pin icon next to Pin-Tube.

Look for the puzzle icon in your toolbar →
2

Watch a video and pin it

While watching any YouTube video, click the pin icon in the video player controls to save it.

3

Find it on your homepage

Pinned videos appear at the top of your YouTube homepage. Click to continue watching instantly.
